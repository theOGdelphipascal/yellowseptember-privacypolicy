Yellow September – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

This policy explains what information is involved when you play Yellow September (“the game”) on iPhone or iPad, who handles it, and the choices you have. The game is made by Fraser Hughes (“I”, “me”), an independent developer based in the United Kingdom.

The short version

  • There are no accounts, and I don't collect your name, email or location.
  • Your game progress is saved only on your device.
  • Ads are provided by Google AdMob, which collects some device information to show and measure ads. In the UK and EU you're asked for consent first.
  • Purchases are handled entirely by Apple. I never see your payment details.
  • If you buy “No Ads”, the game stops loading ads altogether.

1. Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress locally on your device so you can carry on where you left off. This includes:

  • coins, best distance and other game statistics;
  • upgrades, submarine skins, keys and treasure chests you own;
  • whether you have purchased “No Ads”;
  • a record of recent purchase transaction IDs, so that a coin purchase is never credited twice.

This information stays on your device and is not sent to me. It may be included in your device's iCloud or computer backups, which are controlled by Apple and by you. Deleting the game deletes this data from your device.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game can show optional rewarded video ads, which you choose to watch at the end of a dive in exchange for double coins. These ads are provided by Google AdMob, a service of Google.

What Google may collect

  • device information, such as model, operating system version, language and screen size;
  • your IP address, which can indicate your approximate location;
  • advertising identifiers. On iOS, Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA) is only available if you allow tracking in your device settings. The game itself does not ask for tracking permission;
  • information about ads shown and how you interact with them, such as views, taps and whether a video was completed.

Why it's used

Google uses this information to deliver and measure ads, limit how often you see the same ad, prevent fraud, and, where you have agreed, show ads that are more relevant to you (personalised ads). Google acts as an independent controller of the data it collects. You can read how Google uses information from apps that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and in Google's Privacy Policy.

Your consent

If you're in the United Kingdom, the European Economic Area or Switzerland, the game shows a consent message from Google's User Messaging Platform before any ads are requested. You can accept, refuse, or choose which purposes you agree to. If you don't consent to personalised ads, you may still see non-personalised ads, which are based on general context rather than your activity. To change your choice, you can delete and reinstall the game to see the consent message again, or use the privacy settings option in the game where one is provided.

“No Ads” purchase

If you purchase “No Ads”, the game no longer loads or shows ads, and you automatically receive the double-coins reward instead.

3. In-app purchases (Apple)

Coin packs and “No Ads” are sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment and handles your billing information under Apple's Privacy Policy. I do not receive your name, email address, card details or Apple Account details. The game receives only a confirmation from Apple of which item was bought, along with a transaction ID. It uses these to deliver your coins or unlock “No Ads”, and to restore “No Ads” if you reinstall.

4. Crash reports

If you have chosen in your device settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may send me anonymous crash reports and usage statistics. These don't identify you and are used only to fix problems. The game does not include any other analytics or tracking tools.

6. Children

Yellow September is not directed at children under 13, and I don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, please contact me and I'll help resolve it.

7. International transfers

Google and Apple may process information in countries outside the UK, including the United States. They use recognised safeguards for these transfers, such as the UK International Data Transfer Addendum and standard contractual clauses.

8. How long information is kept

Game data stays on your device until you delete the game. Google and Apple keep the information they collect in line with their own privacy policies, linked above. I don't hold any personal data about you on my own systems.

9. Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to access, correct or delete personal data held about you, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to withdraw consent. As I don't hold personal data about you myself, most requests about ads or purchases are best directed to Google or Apple. I'm still happy to help with any question.

If you're unhappy with how your information is handled, you can complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

If the game changes in a way that affects your privacy, for example by adding new features or services, I'll update this page and the “Last updated” date above.
